# Vision

> «Я всегда хотел, чтобы песня звучала как вечер. Чтобы не мешала думать, а помогала».
> — Женя Мычак

## 1. What this is

**«Станция Видеопарк»** is a digital archive and musical memorial for the Brovary bedroom/minimal-wave project
**Станция Видеопарк** (2016–2020) and its author **Женя Мычак** (1999–2020), who wrote, played, recorded and mixed
almost everything at home on a Squier Bullet Strat, an iPhone 6 and a computer.

It is a **place you visit and stay in for a while** — not a page you read. The visitor does not "read Yevhenii Mychak's
biography"; they spend an evening inside his artistic world: songs that sound like evenings, rain, forest, spring,
street lamps, windows, dreams, and the suburban quiet of Brovary.

The name itself is the concept. Zhenya heard a little boy in the Kyiv metro shout *«Станция Видеопарк!»* as the train
pulled into **Гидропарк**. A misheard station became a band. We turn it back into a station: the visitor arrives,
looks at the line map, and chooses where to ride next. (The story of the name is told as its own scene in История.)

## 2. Why it exists

1. **Keep the music audible.** Most of the catalogue appeared posthumously as scattered archival releases across
   SoundCloud, Bandcamp and streaming services. The site gathers it into one coherent, beautiful, listenable whole.
2. **Introduce new listeners.** Someone who heard «Плавится лёд» once should be able to fall into the whole world.
3. **Remember Zhenya as an artist,** through his work, words and images — with dignity, warmth and accuracy.
4. **Be an archive of record.** Correct dates (recorded vs released), versions, credits, sources, lyrics — a reference
   for fans, journalists and researchers of the late-2010s DIY / lo-fi scene.

## 3. Audiences

| Audience | Arrives with | Needs | Success looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| **New listener** (link from a friend, playlist, Last.fm, a TikTok/Reels clip) | Curiosity, 30 seconds of attention | Immediate atmosphere, one tap to music, no wall of text | Stays 5+ minutes, plays several tracks, opens Lyrics or Photos |
| **Existing fan** | Love for the music, nostalgia | Complete, well-organised catalogue; lyrics; rare material | Returns; shares a lyric card |
| **Friends & family** | Grief and pride | A respectful, accurate, warm portrait | Feel Zhenya is represented as he was |
| **Researcher / journalist** | A question | Verified facts, dates, sources, credits | Finds and cites a fact with its source in < 1 minute |
| **Fellow musicians / label (Raw Pop Syndicate)** | Professional interest | Correct credits, links to official releases | Releases correctly credited and linked |

Primary language at launch: Russian (the language of the songs). Ukrainian and English follow (see [i18n.md](i18n.md)).

## 4. How it should feel

**Keywords:** evening · quiet · alive · warm · nostalgic · intimate · homemade · poetic · unhurried · luminous.

**Anti-keywords:** funeral · gothic · tragic · glossy · corporate · "rock band merch site" · encyclopedic · busy · loud.

The emotional arc of a visit:

1. **Arrival** — curiosity and calm. A silent platform at dusk, a train arriving, the station lamp lighting the name «Станция Видеопарк».
2. **Listening** — warmth. The visitor presses play; the music *keeps playing* wherever they go.
3. **Exploring** — discovery. Photos, handwritten-feeling lyrics, Brovary streets, the boy in the metro who misheard «Гидропарк».
4. **Understanding** — the story assembles itself: Pink Floyd at eight, «Грустные товарищи», the metro, the home recordings
   (the guitar, the phone — met inside the biography), the label.
5. **Remembering** — only now, gently, the visitor learns how short the story was. The music continues anyway —
   it was still being released after August 2020, and it is still playing in their player.
6. **Leaving** — a quiet warmth, a song in your head, a lyric shared.

The death is **known but not displayed**. We never open with it; we never decorate with it; we never hide it.

## 5. Principles

1. **Presence over absence.** Show what he made, not what was lost.
2. **Music never stops.** Navigation never interrupts playback.
3. **Place, not pages.** Every section is a station with its own atmosphere, not a template with different text.
4. **Progressive disclosure.** Atmosphere → music → fragments → story → the full biography.
5. **Slowness is a feature.** Evening tempo: slow fades, long holds, space. But never slow to *respond*.
6. **Homemade honesty.** The aesthetics of a bedroom recording: imperfect, textured, real — not fake grunge.
7. **Truth with sources.** An archive is only as good as its accuracy. Uncertainty is shown, not smoothed over.
8. **Everyone can enter.** Phones first — most visitors arrive from a shared link on mobile. Reduced-motion, screen readers,
   slow phones on mobile data, no sound — all first-class.
9. **The words are his.** Lyrics and quotes are verbatim; we frame them, we don't rewrite them.

## 6. Reference projects and what we take from them

| Reference | What we borrow | What we avoid |
|---|---|---|
| [Prince Online Museum](https://www.princeonlinemuseum.com/) | Timeline as the primary navigation spine; eras as rooms; immersive spaces | Scale and heaviness; museum-institutional tone |
| [Avicii — Memory Board](https://avicii.com/) | Warmth of a collective portrait. A memory board is **deferred** (not in current scope), but the architecture leaves room for it | Unmoderated volume; celebrity scale |
| [Aunty Archibald](https://auntyarchibald.com/) | Turning a big SoundCloud archive into an *own* music interface; the player as the centre of the site | Dependency on a third-party player UI |

## 7. Signature ideas

These are the ideas that make the site *this* site. Details in [information-architecture.md](information-architecture.md) and [design.md](design.md).

1. **The arrival.** The first screen is a platform at dusk. A single button — «Войти на станцию» — and a train arrives
   in a sweep of light, the doors open. The arrival is **silent**: entering never turns on sound. Music starts only when the visitor
   presses play; ambient sounds only via the explicit «Звук» toggle.
2. **The wordmark.** «Станция Видеопарк» set like a station name plate on the red line — a calm, crafted wordmark that lights up
   like a platform lamp. See [design.md §5](design.md#5-signature-components).
3. **Г → В, in История.** History opens with the scene of how the name was born, told from Zhenya's interview: a metro car,
   the sign «Гидропарк», a child's shout «Станция Видеопарк!» — and the «Г» on the sign flips to «В».
4. **The line map.** Navigation is a metro line diagram in muted Kyiv-red (the real M1 line runs through Гидропарк toward
   Brovary). Moving forward along the line slides content like a passing train.
5. **The persistent player.** A small player bar lives across all stations and survives navigation. Queue, waveforms,
   lyrics in sync where available. On phones it is a thumb-reachable bottom bar with a full-screen sheet.
6. **Two rails of time.** The History timeline has two parallel tracks: *recorded* and *released*. Much of the music was
   recorded in 2016–2019 and released in 2020; showing both rails tells that story visually and honestly.
7. **«Песня как вечер» — Evening mode.** A full-screen listening mode: just the music, a slowly breathing twilight
   gradient, the current lyric line. Also works as a continuous "radio" through the whole archive.
8. **Time of day.** The site's light subtly follows the visitor's local time — dawn, day, evening (the default palette),
   night. Most people will visit in the evening; the site is most itself then.
9. **His own words beyond the songs.** Early poems and prose (2014) and his Instagram captions sit next to the lyrics — the voice of
   a teenager becoming an author. The home studio (Squier Bullet Strat, iPhone 6, a computer) is told in words inside the biography,
   without object photos.
10. **Transfers («Пересадки»).** Every song, release, photo and event links to related entities: poets whose words he sang
    (Desnos, Kormiltsev), poets he loved (Verlaine, Rimbaud, Baudelaire, Wojaczek, the Silver Age), artists who made covers,
    places in Brovary. Like metro transfers between lines.
11. **Brovary — the terminal station.** A map of the town where everything was recorded: streets, light, field recordings, and
    how to get there (red line to Лісова, then the bus). «Конечная» — and a transfer to Zhenya's story.
12. **«Архив ищет» (the archive is looking).** The tracklist of «Мой милый дом» is lost; some songs have no known release.
    We say so openly and invite people who have files, photos or knowledge to help restore them.
13. **Shareable lyric cards.** Any lyric line can be shared as a generated image (build-time OG images per song).
14. **Track of the evening.** One song is featured each day (deterministic by date), so returning visitors find something new.

## 8. What success means

- Median session ≥ 4 minutes; ≥ 50% of sessions start playback.
- Every release, song and recording in the knowledge base is represented and correctly dated and credited.
- Works beautifully on a phone: every station is fully usable at 360 px, touch-first, portrait and landscape.
- Lighthouse ≥ 95 on Performance/Accessibility/Best Practices/SEO for content pages; WCAG 2.2 AA.
- The site is still online, fast and maintainable in 10 years — static, few dependencies, content in plain files.

## 9. Out of scope (for now)

- Memory board («Воспоминания») — deferred; may return in a later phase.
- Merch shop, ticketing, newsletters, comments under every page.
- User accounts.
- Real-time social feeds rendered client-side.
- Any monetisation.
